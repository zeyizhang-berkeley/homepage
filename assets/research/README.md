# Research illustrations

Eight research-theme illustrations for the homepage. Most are original diagrams using verified public-domain molecular models. At the owner's request, the GPU topic restores the earlier graphene/CO₂ paper panel with a separate GPU symbol. The real-space DFT illustration uses a verified water source within a genuine 4×4×4 cubic Cartesian grid. Both hydrogen display spheres have been reduced to about 60% of their previous diameter through a targeted ImageGen edit. The source molecular view and two O–H single bonds are retained; sphere sizes are schematic. Methane model pixels and proportions are preserved. In the GPU panel, only the label and a tiny carbon-rim region previously obscured by it are edited; all bonds and all pixels outside that local region remain unchanged.

## Source credits

- **Methane:** [Methane-CRC-MW-3D-balls.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Methane-CRC-MW-3D-balls.png), by Ben Mills (Benjah-bmm27), released into the public domain. The source cites microwave spectroscopy and the *CRC Handbook*, 88th edition. The full model has four C–H bonds in the original tetrahedral projection. The same unmodified source is used in the three AI for Science illustrations.

- **Water:** [Water-3D-balls.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Water-3D-balls.png), by Ben Mills (Benjah-bmm27), released into the public domain. The verified source has been edited with built-in ImageGen to make the two hydrogen display spheres smaller, retaining one oxygen, two hydrogens, two O–H single bonds and the original molecular view. This changes display radii, not molecular composition; these sphere sizes do not represent physical atomic radii.
- **Restored GPU illustration:** *Real-space Kohn–Sham density functional theory for complex energy applications*, [DOI: 10.1039/D5CC01820J](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5CC01820J), Figure 9(a). The original graphene/CO₂ panel is cropped without resampling and combined with a separate conceptual GPU; the review caption credits the original source under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The “a)” marker is removed. Built-in ImageGen edits only the small carbon-rim area it obscured; the final composition uses a 32×24 source-pixel repair region and preserves every pixel outside the label-edit regions. All bonds remain unchanged. The diagram is not a GPU performance result.
- **Grid concept:** The new real-space diagram follows the representation of wavefunctions, densities and potentials on regular grids described in the [Octopus grid documentation](https://www.octopus-code.org/documentation/main/developers/code_documentation/grids/). The diagram is newly drawn; no documentation image is copied.
- **Spectroscopy concept:** The new diagram connects many-electron excitation energies with qualitative broadened absorption bands, consistent with the [ORCA spectrum-plotting documentation](https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.1/manual/contents/utilitiesvisualization/utilities.html#orca-mapspc). Larger excitation energy corresponds to a peak farther right on an energy axis. No documentation or paper image is copied.

[sources.json](sources.json) records source hashes, geometry checks, conceptual references, composition methods, and asset hashes. [prompts.json](prompts.json) records the built-in ImageGen prompts for the non-molecular QMC illustration, localized GPU label repair, and water hydrogen-size edit. The real-space grid and density styling remain native SVG; the water is adapted from the verified source. Earlier generated molecular drafts and superseded variants are archived outside the current site; the GPU paper-panel composition is the explicitly requested exception.

These are research-theme illustrations, not new calculated results. Matrices, arrows, spectra, density envelopes, and occupation diagrams are conceptual. The water-density envelope is not a computed density or a signed orbital. The excitation diagram represents many-electron states, not Kohn–Sham orbital energies; its spectral widths and heights are illustrative. The multireference diagram shows two paired configurations without assigning energies, coefficients, or relative phase; it is not a complete active-space expansion. The QMC image depicts electronic-state walkers and auxiliary-field propagation, not atom trajectories.

The SVG files contain both vector graphics and source raster panels; the original panels' resolution is retained. Illustrations remain to the right of their text at every screen width as static images, without links or click-to-enlarge behavior. Narrow screens use a smaller image column while keeping the text on the left. Edit each topic's `image` object in `data/profile.json` to select a different asset or alternative text.
