# Research illustrations

Nine research-theme illustrations for the homepage. Most are original diagrams using verified public-domain molecular models. At the owner's request, the GPU topic restores the earlier graphene/CO₂ paper panel with a separate GPU symbol. The real-space DFT illustration renders a six-water prism from published Cartesian coordinates inside a wider rectangular real-space domain. It preserves all six water molecules and twelve O–H single bonds, with smaller white hydrogen spheres and fine dashed intermolecular hydrogen bonds. The atom spheres, mesh and density styling are native SVG. Methane source pixels remain unchanged. In the GPU panel, only the label and a tiny carbon-rim region previously obscured by it are edited; all bonds and all pixels outside that local region remain unchanged.

## Source credits

- **Methane:** [Methane-CRC-MW-3D-balls.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Methane-CRC-MW-3D-balls.png), by Ben Mills (Benjah-bmm27), released into the public domain. The source cites microwave spectroscopy and the *CRC Handbook*, 88th edition. The full model has four C–H bonds in the original tetrahedral projection. The same unmodified source is used in the three AI for Science illustrations.

- **Water cluster:** [Cambridge Cluster Database](https://www-wales.ch.cam.ac.uk/~wales/CCD/anant-watcl.html), [6-prism XYZ](https://www-wales.ch.cam.ac.uk/~wales/CCD/anant/xyz/6-prism.xyz). The database reports RHF/6-31G(d,p) geometries and cites [Maheshwary et al., J. Phys. Chem. A 105, 10525–10537 (2001)](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp013141b). The [local XYZ file](water-hexamer-prism.xyz) preserves the original coordinates. Native SVG renders the 6 oxygen and 12 hydrogen atoms with 12 solid O–H bonds; selected geometric hydrogen-bond contacts appear as thin dashed lines. Sphere radii are a display convention, not physical atomic radii. This is original rendering from scientific coordinates, not a copied paper image or an AI-generated molecular geometry.
- **Restored GPU illustration:** *Real-space Kohn–Sham density functional theory for complex energy applications*, [DOI: 10.1039/D5CC01820J](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5CC01820J), Figure 9(a). The original graphene/CO₂ panel is cropped without resampling and combined with a separate conceptual GPU; the review caption credits the original source under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The “a)” marker is removed. Built-in ImageGen edits only the small carbon-rim area it obscured; the final composition uses a 32×24 source-pixel repair region and preserves every pixel outside the label-edit regions. All bonds remain unchanged. The diagram is not a GPU performance result.
- **Grid concept:** The real-space diagram follows the representation of wavefunctions, densities and potentials on regular grids described in the [Octopus grid documentation](https://www.octopus-code.org/documentation/main/developers/code_documentation/grids/). The diagram is newly drawn; no documentation image is copied.
- **Spectroscopy concept:** The new diagram connects many-electron excitation energies with qualitative broadened absorption bands, consistent with the [ORCA spectrum-plotting documentation](https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.1/manual/contents/utilitiesvisualization/utilities.html#orca-mapspc). Larger excitation energy corresponds to a peak farther right on an energy axis. No documentation or paper image is copied.

[sources.json](sources.json) records source hashes, geometry checks, conceptual references, composition methods, and asset hashes. [prompts.json](prompts.json) records the built-in ImageGen prompts for the non-molecular QMC illustration, localized GPU label repair, and archived single-water edit. The active water cluster and its rectangular mesh are native SVG derived from the published XYZ geometry. Earlier generated molecular drafts and superseded variants are archived outside the current site; the GPU paper-panel composition is the explicitly requested exception.

These are research-theme illustrations, not new calculated results. Matrices, arrows, spectra, density envelopes, and occupation diagrams are conceptual. The water-density envelope is not a computed density or a signed orbital. The excitation diagram represents many-electron states, not Kohn–Sham orbital energies; its spectral widths and heights are illustrative. The multireference diagram shows two paired configurations without assigning energies, coefficients, or relative phase; it is not a complete active-space expansion. The QMC image depicts electronic-state walkers and auxiliary-field propagation, not atom trajectories.

Some SVG files embed source raster panels; their original resolution is retained. The current real-space water-cluster diagram is fully vector and remains sharp when scaled. Illustrations remain to the right of their text at every screen width as static images, without links or click-to-enlarge behavior. Narrow screens use a smaller image column while keeping the text on the left. Edit each topic's `image` object in `data/profile.json` to select a different asset or alternative text.

- **Relativistic effects with spin–orbit coupling:** Original vector artwork in `relativistic-effects-soc.svg` separates a representative scalar effect from SOC. A diffuse gray nonrelativistic (NR) s-density envelope and a contracted teal scalar-relativistic (SR) envelope depict qualitative s-orbital contraction; symbolic L and S vectors depict spin–orbit coupling. Both panels are relativistic effects. The drawing does not imply that all orbitals contract, represent electron trajectories, specify quantum numbers, or show calculated densities for a particular complex. Scientific basis: [ORCA relativistic-effects documentation](https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.1/manual/contents/spectroscopyproperties/xes.html#inclusion-of-relativistic-effects) and [ADF relativistic-effects documentation](https://www.scm.com/doc/ADF/Input/Relativistic_effects.html), checked 2026-10-27. No source image is copied. The earlier SOC-only SVG is retained as an unused asset.
